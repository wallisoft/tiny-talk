# How tiny-talk changes

tiny-talk has a slow core and a quick edge.

## The core: the basic characters' pictures

- Changes come out in **numbered editions** (`core/pictures.txt` says which), no more often than every
  three months, so that what people have learned stays true.
- **A picture never gets a new meaning.** Once 🔥 has meant fire in an edition, it is never given to anything
  else, even if fire moves to another picture. Old messages stay readable.
- A suggestion for the core needs **10 or more 👍**, and a maintainer's agreement that it reads better for most
  people, not just for one language or culture.

## The edge: words of their own

- A whole word may be given its own picture (`edge/pictures.txt`): 🐈 rather than "animal + claws".
- A suggestion with **5 or more 👍** and no reasoned objection after 7 days is added by a maintainer.

## Words

- Corrections and additions to `words/` are welcome at any time, by pull request or suggestion.
- For a word with several translations, the one with the most 👍 goes first.

## Who decides

The maintainers, for now Steve Wallis (Wallisoft). Votes guide them; they don't bind them, because a picture
must work for everyone, including people who never vote. Disagreements are settled in the open, on the issue.
