# Taking part

Three ways, from no effort to a little:

## 1. Vote 👍

Every suggestion is a GitHub issue. Give the ones you like a 👍 (the thumbs-up reaction on the issue itself,
not a comment saying +1). The [vote page](https://tiny-talk.world/vote.html) shows them, most votes first,
and is counted again every day.

## 2. Suggest

From any word on [tiny-talk.world](https://tiny-talk.world), press **Suggest a picture** or
**Suggest a word**: a form opens here with the word already filled in. Or open one yourself:

- [Suggest a picture](../../issues/new?template=picture.yml): a better emoji for a basic character, or a picture
  of its own for a whole word.
- [Suggest a word](../../issues/new?template=word.yml): a missing word, a better one, or a correction, in any of
  the 19 languages.

A good picture is **old enough to show on most phones** (Unicode 12 or earlier), **shows in colour without
extra codes**, is **one emoji, not a sequence**, and is **not already used** for something else.

## 3. Edit the files

Words are plain text: `words/fr.txt` holds `12345=mot;autre mot`. Fix or add a line and open a pull request.
Several words for one symbol are separated by `;`, the most usual first. Please change one language per pull
request and say where your word comes from if it isn't everyday.

Pictures in `core/` change only in a new edition (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)), so for those, suggest
rather than edit.

## Be kind

Suggestions are about pictures and words, never about people. Assume the other person's language works
differently from yours, because it does.
